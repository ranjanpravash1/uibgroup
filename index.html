Who We Are

A Trusted Name in Engineering Consultancy

UB Group is a wholly-owned proprietary firm established in 2025, headquartered in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. We are one of the prominent consultancy firms engaged in offering a wide range of engineering services across India.

Our services span Manpower Supply, AutoCAD Design, Supply of Stone, Aggregates & Sand, and the timely completion of Civil projects. With a specialized in-house talent pool and the ability to deliver holistic solutions under one roof, we stand distinctively apart.

We develop long-term, fruitful relationships with clients by offering cost-effective services, adopting ethical, sensible, and transparent business policies to earn and retain client trust.
